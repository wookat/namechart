# NameChart Press Kit (source of truth: /press page)

## Boilerplate (short)
NameChart charts 146 years of official U.S. baby name data — popularity curves, meanings, famous namesakes and sibling-name matching for 105,000+ names. No ads, no account; free during Beta.

## Boilerplate (long)
NameChart is a data-first baby name explorer for expectant parents. Every one of its 105,954 names has a full 1880–2025 popularity chart built from the U.S. Social Security Administration's public dataset, plus documented meanings (Wiktionary, CC BY-SA), famous namesakes (Wikidata, CC0), state-by-state rankings, head-to-head comparisons, a data-driven Baby Name Generator, and a Sibling & Middle Name Matcher. Shortlists live in the browser — no account — and can be shared with revocable links. The site sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers. NameChart is currently in free Beta and is a Zalize project.
