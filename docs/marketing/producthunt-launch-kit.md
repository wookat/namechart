## First comment (maker comment)
Hi PH! 👋 We built NameChart because picking a baby name felt like reading ad-stuffed listicles instead of looking at facts.

NameChart gives every one of 105,954 names a full popularity chart from 146 years of official U.S. birth records — plus documented meanings, famous namesakes, state-by-state maps, and two tools we're proud of:
- **Sibling & Middle Name Matcher** — enter names you love, get sibling names from the same era & popularity tier, and middle names that flow.
- **Shareable shortlists** — save names with ♡ (no account), share a link with your partner, revoke it anytime.

No ads, no cookies, no signup. Everything is free during the Beta. Would love your feedback — especially what data you'd want next (we're eyeing international rankings).

## FAQ answers
- **How is this different from Nameberry?** Data-first: full historical charts for every name are free and ad-free; Nameberry gates several data features behind a subscription.
- **Where does the data come from?** U.S. SSA public dataset (1880–2025), Wiktionary (CC BY-SA) for meanings, Wikidata (CC0) for namesakes. Names with <5 babies/year are excluded at the source.
- **Business model?** Free during Beta; published Plus ($4) / Pro ($9) plans later, with clear notice before any billing.
- **Privacy?** No cookies, no trackers; anonymous first-party page counts only.
